Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-30 · Operated by {{ operator_name }}

1. Who is responsible for your data

{{ operator_name }} operates this platform and is the data controller for studio account and billing data. Each studio is the data controller for the data they collect about their own clients (booking details, galleries, invoices); we process that data on the studio's instructions as a data processor.

2. What we collect

  • Account data: studio name, owner name, email, password hash, plan and billing status.
  • Operational data studios enter: listings, clients, appointments, invoices, galleries.
  • Media uploaded by studios on behalf of their clients.
  • Technical data: IP addresses and timestamps for security, rate limiting, and abuse prevention.

3. How we use it

We use data to provide and secure the service, process subscriptions, send transactional email (account verification, delivery notifications, receipts), and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal data and do not use studio media for advertising or model training.

4. Subprocessors

Payments are processed by Stripe (subscription billing and studio payouts); Stripe's own privacy policy applies to payment data. Transactional email is sent through the configured email provider. Media may be stored in encrypted object storage (S3-compatible) when enabled by the operator. We do not otherwise share personal data with third parties except as required by law.

5. Cookies

We use strictly-necessary cookies only: session sign-in, CSRF protection, and gallery access tokens. No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies are set.

6. Retention & deletion

Account and studio data is kept while the account is active and deleted within a reasonable window after termination. Security logs and rate-limit records are kept briefly and then purged. Studios can delete listings, clients, and media at any time from their admin area.

7. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the GDPR/UK GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct, export, restrict, or delete your personal data, and to object to processing. Studio owners can exercise most rights directly in the admin area; for anything else, contact us. Clients of a studio should contact that studio first — we act on the studio's instructions for their data.
